What we check

Real evidence. Not guesswork.

Every claim a recruiter makes gets cross-referenced against independent, authoritative sources you can verify yourself.

🏢

Company Registry

Cross-checks company names against UK Companies House, SEC EDGAR, MCA India, and UAE NER registries in real time.

🌐

Domain Age & History

WHOIS and RDAP lookups flag domains under 180 days old — a key indicator of hastily-constructed scam infrastructure.

🤖

ML Fraud Detection

Binary + multiclass ML models trained on real fraud data detect advance-fee patterns, urgency signals, and credential harvesting.

🔗

URL Reputation

PhishTank database lookups and ML URL analysis identify known phishing sites and suspicious domain patterns.

📧

Email Domain Mismatch

Flags recruiter emails using free providers (Gmail, Yahoo) when claiming to represent large registered enterprises.

🛡️

Meeting Link Inspector

Pre-call inspection of meeting invites for typosquats, unsolicited channels, lookalikes, and coercion signals — without joining.

How it works

Three checks. No black box.

Every claim is checked against a source the recruiter doesn't control — and you see exactly what was found.

📋
01 / CLAIM

Extract the Claim

Company name, registration, job location, and payment terms are pulled from the posting — nothing assumed.

🔍
02 / CHECK

Verify Independently

Each claim is checked against a source the recruiter doesn't control — registry, domain record, trained fraud model.

📊
03 / RESULT

Show the Receipt

You get a plain result with its source and date — verified, unverifiable, or contradicted — never a guess dressed up as certainty.
